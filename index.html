Who are WE?

HIV/AIDS Awareness Project and Youth Development (HAPYD) is a non-profit organisation (NPO) that is focused on empowering, growing and equipping youth, giving necessary support and resources to orphaned and vulnerable children and youth headed households engaging and helping families that are infected and affected by HIV/AIDS. Our project has five different programmes namely Psychosocial Support, Youth Development Programmes, Home Based Care Support, Educational Support, Referrals and Linkage Services.

Our Vision

To address the issues that are related to HIV/AIDS and to ensure that vulnerable and marginalised communities are treated with care, respect, dignity and understanding.

Our Mission

To equip and empower vulnerable and marginalised communities with a variety of skills through the provision of a comprehensive package of services to live self-sufficient lives free from discrimination.
